# Small Business Financing Email Templates

## Overview
Complete cold email templates and sequences for small business financing outreach.

## Template 1: Initial Outreach
**Subject:** Quick question about your business financing
**Body:** Personalized introduction + value proposition

## Template 2: Follow-up (3 days)
**Subject:** Following up on financing options
**Body:** Additional benefits + case study

## Template 3: Value-add (7 days)
**Subject:** Free financing assessment
**Body:** Offer free analysis + testimonials

## Template 4: Final attempt (14 days)
**Subject:** Last chance for special offer
**Body:** Limited-time offer + urgency

## Template 5: Breakup (21 days)
**Subject:** Closing your file
**Body:** Polite closure + future availability

## Compliance
- CAN-SPAM compliant
- GDPR considerations included
- Opt-out instructions clear
- No misleading claims
