## Compliance
- CAN-SPAM compliant: physical address, unsubscribe link, clear subject lines
- GDPR: consent checkbox on lead capture
- TCPA: no automated calls unless opted in
- Truth in Lending: all rates disclosed as APR ranges, not guarantees
- Opt-out instructions in every email footer
- No misleading claims about approval guarantees
