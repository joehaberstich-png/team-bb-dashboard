East Coast Australia Itinerary

Sydney to Cairns — the ultimate East Coast adventure

Overview

Duration: 2-3 weeks | Distance: ~2,500km | Best Time: April-October

The East Coast of Australia is a rite of passage for travelers. This itinerary takes you from Sydney's iconic harbor all the way up to Cairns, gateway to the Great Barrier Reef and Daintree Rainforest.

Suggested Route

  • Sydney (3 days): Opera House, Bondi Beach, Harbour Bridge climb, The Rocks
  • Byron Bay (2 days): Lighthouse walk, surf lessons, local markets
  • Gold Coast (2 days): Surfers Paradise, theme parks, Burleigh Heads
  • Brisbane (1 day): South Bank, eat street markets, Story Bridge
  • Fraser Island (2 days): 4WD tour, Lake McKenzie, Maheno Wreck
  • Airlie Beach/Whitsundays (3 days): Whitehaven Beach, Hill Inlet, snorkeling
  • Cairns (3 days): Great Barrier Reef, Daintree Rainforest, Kuranda Skyrail

Budget Estimate

Backpacker: $1,500-$2,500 AUD | Mid-range: $3,000-$5,000 AUD | Comfort: $5,000+ AUD
