Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about traveling to Australia

Visas

Do I need a visa to visit Australia?

Yes. All visitors to Australia need a visa or Electronic Travel Authority (ETA) before traveling. The type depends on your nationality and purpose of visit.

How long can I stay on a tourist visa?

Most tourist visas allow stays of 3, 6, or 12 months. The ETA (Subclass 601) allows stays of up to 3 months per visit.

Costs

How much does a trip to Australia cost?

A 2-week trip typically costs $3,000-$6,000 AUD for mid-range travel including flights, accommodation, food, and activities.

Is Australia expensive?

Major cities like Sydney and Melbourne are comparable to New York or London. Regional areas are more affordable.

Best Time to Visit

When is the best time to visit Australia?

It depends on the region. Generally, April-October offers pleasant weather across most of the country. December-February is peak summer and holiday season.
