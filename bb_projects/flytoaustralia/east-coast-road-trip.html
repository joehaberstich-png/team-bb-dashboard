2-Week East Coast Australia Road Trip

The ultimate itinerary from Sydney to Cairns

The East Coast of Australia is one of the world's greatest road trips. From the iconic Sydney Opera House to the ancient Daintree Rainforest, this 2-week itinerary covers the best stops along the Pacific Coast.

At a Glance

  • Route: Sydney → Byron Bay → Gold Coast → Fraser Island → Whitsundays → Cairns
  • Distance: ~2,500 km
  • Best Time: April-October (dry season)
  • Budget: $2,500-$4,000 AUD (mid-range)

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Days 1-3: Sydney

Start in Australia's most famous city. Visit the Opera House, Bondi Beach, and take the ferry to Manly. Don't miss the coastal walk from Bondi to Coogee.

Days 4-5: Byron Bay

Drive 8 hours north to Byron Bay — Australia's bohemian beach town. Watch the sunrise from the Cape Byron lighthouse, take a surfing lesson, and explore the local markets.

Days 6-7: Gold Coast & Brisbane

Surfers Paradise for theme parks and nightlife, then Brisbane for culture and the South Bank precinct.

Days 8-9: Fraser Island (K'Gari)

The world's largest sand island. Book a 4WD tour to see Lake McKenzie, the Maheno Shipwreck, and the Champagne Pools.

Days 10-11: Whitsundays & Airlie Beach

Sail to Whitehaven Beach and Heart Reef. This is the gateway to the Great Barrier Reef — book a snorkeling or diving trip.

Days 12-14: Cairns & The Daintree

End in tropical Far North Queensland. Take the Kuranda Skyrail, explore the Daintree Rainforest, and do a reef trip from Cairns.

Transport Tips

Consider buying a Greyhound Australia hop-on-hop-off pass, or rent a campervan for maximum flexibility. If flying between cities, Jetstar and Virgin Australia have frequent domestic flights.
