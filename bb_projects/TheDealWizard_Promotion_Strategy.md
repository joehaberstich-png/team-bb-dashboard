$500 Amazon gift card contest promotion strategy to drive awareness and traffic to TheDealWizard.com.

1. **Reddit Communities** - r/beermoney, r/sweepstakes
2. **Facebook Groups** - Contest & Giveaway groups
3. **Twitter Hashtags** - #giveaway #contest #amazongiftcard
4. **Partnership Outreach** - Related websites/blogs

- Facebook boosted posts: $30

## Timeline
- Week 1: Setup & initial posts
- Week 2: Partnership outreach

- Week 4: Final promotion & winner selection

- 500+ contest entries
- 50+ website signups
