## 1. Market Overview (2026)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| US addiction treatment market | $12.4B (2026), growing 5-6% CAGR |
| Behavioral health market | $96.9B (2025), projected $159B by 2035 |

### Key Trends
- **Telehealth permanency:** DEA extended telemedicine flexibilities for controlled substance prescribing (buprenorphine) through 2025+ — this is HUGE for suboxone doctor matching
- **Opioid settlement money:** $50B flowing to states for treatment infrastructure — more providers entering the space
- **Insurance coverage:** Most states now require insurance to cover addiction treatment (ACA parity)
- **Workforce shortage:** 40M+ Americans in mental health shortage areas — provider directories are essential

| Competitor | Monthly Traffic | Revenue Model | Estimated Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| PsychologyToday.com | ~60M | Paid listings ($30/mo per therapist) | $50-100M/yr |

### Opportunity for DrugDoctors.com
The addiction treatment space is **fragmented**. SAMHSA's free directory is clunky. Psychology Today is broad (mental health, not addiction-specific). Recovery.com focuses on inpatient rehab, **not** outpatient MAT (suboxone) which is where the telehealth boom is.

**DrugDoctors.com can dominate the "find a suboxone doctor near me" niche** — a specific, high-intent, growing segment with no dominant player.

## 2. Revenue Model (3-Phase Ramp)

### Phase 1: Lead Generation (Month 1-3) — $500-2K/mo
- **How it works:** Visitor fills out "Find a Suboxone Doctor Near Me" form → we email/call them with 2-3 provider matches → treatment center pays us per lead
- **Pricing:** $30-50 per qualified lead (cold transfer), $75-150 per warm transfer
- **Lead sources:** SEO + local keyword targeting + Google Maps scraping for provider data
- **Affiliate bonus:** Add QuickMD, Buoy Health, BetterHelp affiliate links on info pages

### Phase 2: Paid Provider Directory (Month 3-6) — $2-10K/mo
- **How it works:** Addiction doctors/treatment centers pay monthly fee for premium listing
- **Pricing:** $49/mo (basic), $99/mo (featured with reviews), $199/mo (top placement + lead alerts)
- **Target:** 50-100 provider listings = $2,500-10K/mo
- **Catch-up to Psychology Today's $30/mo pricing by offering addiction-specific value**

### Phase 3: Telehealth Matching Platform (Month 6-12) — $10K-100K+/mo
- **How it works:** Integrated booking + telehealth scheduling for addiction medicine
- **Pricing:** $20-50 booking fee per appointment
- **Requirements:** HIPAA-compliant backend, provider onboarding, payment processing
- **Exit valuation:** 20-30x monthly revenue at this stage

## 5. Lead Generation System

### Intake Form (Simple)
```
Fields: Name | Phone | Email | Insurance Provider | Primary Substance | Zip Code | Preferred Contact Method
```

### Lead Flow
1. Visitor submits form on DrugDoctors.com
2. Notification goes to our lead management system (simple CRM or Google Sheets)
3. We call the lead within 5 minutes (HIPAA-compliant)
4. Warm transfer to 1-2 local treatment centers
5. Treatment center pays us $30-50 per lead (or $75-150 for warm transfer with phone call verification)

### Lead Qualification Criteria
- ✅ Has insurance (or self-pay)
- ✅ Seeking treatment within 7 days (urgency)
- ✅ Located near available providers
- ❌ Minors (redirect to youth-specific resources)
- ❌ Crisis/emergency (redirect to 988 suicide hotline)

### Lead Buyer Outreach
1. Scrape Google Maps for addiction treatment centers in top 100 cities
2. Cold email: "We have people in your area searching for treatment. Want qualified leads?"
3. Offer: First 5 leads free as trial
4. After trial: $30-50/lead or monthly subscription

## 6. Compliance & Legal

### Required Pages
- Medical Disclaimer (every page footer)
- HIPAA Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Accessibility Statement
- ADA Compliance
- Cookie Consent

### Content Rules
- NO medical advice (always "consult your doctor")
- NO specific medication dosages without disclaimer
- NO guarantees of treatment outcomes
- Fact-check all statistics with government sources (SAMHSA, CDC, NIH)
- Include SAMHSA National Helpline (1-800-662-4357) on every page
- Include 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline on every page

### Business Compliance
- HIPAA BA agreements with any service handling patient data
- TCPA compliance for phone/text outreach
- CAN-SPAM for email marketing
- State-specific telehealth laws vary — consult healthcare attorney
